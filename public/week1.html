Daron drafted a tribe

Not a metaphor. When the 21 split into Savu and Toka, every single one of Daron's five landed on Toka. Not four of five. Five of five. Her exposure to the purple tribe is zero, which is a remarkable thing to achieve by accident across a five-round snake draft where nobody knew the tribes existed yet.

Toka then lost the first immunity challenge and went to Tribal Council. So in one episode Daron's roster went from perfectly concentrated to perfectly exposed, and she is the only one of the four who lost anybody.

Cagney, meanwhile, drew almost the mirror image — four of five on Savu, the tribe that won. Will came out the most balanced at three and two. Mike sits at two and three. Nobody planned any of this.

The part that is genuinely unfair

Toka's vote was heading for Jenna Doore, who is Will's. Then Patt Cannaday — Daron's — warned her it was coming. Jenna took that information straight to Brady Booker, also Daron's, and told Brady that Aaliyah Puglia had been targeting them. Aaliyah is also Daron's.

So: Daron's player tipped off Will's player, who redirected the vote onto Daron's player, using Daron's other player to do it. Will's pick was saved by Daron's pick at the direct expense of Daron's pick. Three of the four people in that sentence belong to the same person, and she is not the one who benefited.

Elsewhere on the beach

Lewis Kelly, the Irish farmer taken first overall, volunteered for Exile Island before the tribes were even announced — safe from the first vote, but out of the challenge and out of Tribal. The consolation was a shot at a hidden idol, which meant chopping through a mast against a sand timer. The mast won. So the number one pick spent the premiere alone on a different island, swinging an axe, and came back with nothing.

Brady Booker, the professional wrestler, went hard enough at the immunity challenge to injure an achilles. Also Daron's.

And Cristian Chavez, the head of HR nobody drafted, is sitting on Savu having won the first immunity challenge and never faced a vote — doing better than several people who were actually selected.

Voted out · Day 3

Aaliyah Puglia

Chef, 24, Toka — drafted 8th by Daron. Questioned over her effort in the immunity challenge, then caught in the Jenna reversal. Both she and Jenna played a Shot in the Dark and neither one worked.
