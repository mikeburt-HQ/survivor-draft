Draft night · 17 September 2026

Twenty-one names,
twenty picks

Four drafters went through the Survivor 51 cast on Thursday night. Here is how it actually went.

Daron 1st pick

Daron had first pick and immediately took an Irish farmer who lives in Puerto Rico, which is either a power move or a man drafting purely on vibes. He spent the rest of the night assembling chaos: a federal prosecutor, a professional wrestler, a chef and a sociology professor. Somewhere between a courtroom and a bar fight.

Will 2nd pick

Will drafted Canada. Both Canadians in this cast — a game designer and a voice actress — and he took one with his very first pick and the other with his very last, like he had been saving a seat. Round that out with a wedding photographer, a personal trainer and an OBGYN resident and he has comfortably the most functional group chat of the four.

Cagney 3rd pick

Cagney decided to draft adults. His five average 38 years old, a full decade older than Daron's, and include a crisis manager, a mental health counselor and a criminal defense attorney. If anything goes wrong on that beach, that man is staffed for it. He also took the oldest person in the cast, 49-year-old Kristin, and somehow both Rhode Islanders.

Mike 4th pick

Mike went the opposite direction and drafted a farm with a youth movement attached. Two of the three farmers out there, plus three of the four youngest people in the cast — nobody on his roster is over 32 except Maggie, who is 40 and, naturally, also a farmer. He also drafted a guy named Mike, which we are all choosing to believe was strategic.

Best subplot of the night

Daron has the federal prosecutor. Cagney has the criminal defense attorney. Those two have never met and are already on opposite sides.

Undrafted

Cristian Chavez

26, head of HR. Twenty-one castaways, twenty picks, and this group collectively decided it would rather not get called into HR. Brutal.
