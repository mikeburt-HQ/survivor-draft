Rob Antonson got what he wanted

Last week a producer had to interrupt Rob Antonson in the voting booth to explain that you cannot vote for yourself. This week Rob left anyway.

He had hurt a foot days earlier and hidden it. He slept alone on the beach after Tribal while his tribemates openly doubted the injury. The medic looked, found a cut, and said it was not enough to pull him from the game. Rob asked whether it could get infected. The doctor said anything can. Rob went home on day six, and Jeff Probst was careful to say he was not being removed — he was choosing to go.

He is Cagney's, which means Cagney's roster has now produced a rule clarification and a voluntary exit in consecutive weeks. He also never played the immunity idol he was carrying. Keep hold of that fact for about four paragraphs.

Carter Krull missed the boat. Literally.

The episode sent one castaway from each tribe to visit the other. Devin Way went from Toka to Savu, found a hidden advantage in the form of a secret extra vote, and caught his ride home. Carter Krull went from Savu to Toka, was still off searching for an advantage when the boat left, and therefore became a member of Toka.

Not a mutiny. Not a strategic defection. He was simply not there when the boat went. Carter is Mike's, so Mike's roster relocated a man across the map by being slightly too thorough.

And this is where Rob's unplayed idol went: Carter is holding it. One roster lost a player and his idol in the same episode, and the idol crossed to a different roster entirely.

Daron is down to three

Toka lost immunity and went to Tribal, where Patt Cannaday was voted out after Thien An Nguyen worked the players outside the main alliance. Patt is Daron's — and Patt is the one who tipped Jenna Doore off in week one, which is what redirected that vote onto Daron's Aaliyah Puglia in the first place.

So both of Daron's losses trace back through the same person, and now that person is gone too. She has three left, all on Toka, and she still holds Jelly Loblack's idol.

Mike has not lost anybody, and holds half the hardware

Three weeks in, three drafters have taken a casualty and Mike has taken none. All five picks are intact. Two of the four advantages known to be in play belong to Mike's castaways: Carter's inherited idol, and an advantage Maggie Nestor found hidden in Carter's own bag, which she agreed to use jointly with Brady Booker.

Meanwhile Cristian Chavez, still undrafted, is still on Savu and has now outlasted four people who were actually selected.
