Mike's own castaway armed the vote

Maggie Nestor drew the journey, came back with a second extra vote on top of the one already in hand, and gave both of them away — one to Danny Kilby, one to Lewis Kelly, both on Dina, with instructions to use them on Linnea Capobianco. Both were played exactly that way.

Maggie Nestor is Mike's twelfth pick. Linnea Capobianco was Mike's thirteenth. Four episodes and thirty-four ballots went by without a single vote being cast against anybody on that roster, and the run ended with one of Mike's castaways handing out the weapons and naming the target.

Daron came within one refusal of losing Lewis

Dina's vote came back 4–4 between Lewis Kelly and Linnea Capobianco — eight ballots from six people, because both of Maggie's extra votes landed on Linnea. The revote deadlocked as well, which put rocks on the table for everybody not tied.

Danny Kilby would not move off Lewis and was willing to draw. Eric Macksoud, Sharonda Cox and Cristian Chavez came across to Linnea rather than risk it, and nobody drew a rock. So Daron's first-overall pick survived a tie, Mike's thirteenth went home, and the castaway who decided it belongs to Will.

Savu is three people from three different rosters

Alexis Levine's exit leaves Savu at three: Ori Jean-Charles for Will, Jelly Loblack for Daron, Devin Way for Cagney. One each, no repeats, and nobody from Mike — who instead holds four of Toka's six. The smallest tribe and the biggest one are the two cleanest splits on the board.

The prosecution and the defence are both gone

Draft night noted that Cagney had taken criminal defence attorney Alexis Levine at number three while Daron took federal prosecutor Patt Cannaday at number nine, and that they would be on opposite rosters all season. Patt went in episode 3. Alexis went in episode 5. The season is fourteen people deep and neither lawyer made it there.

Cristian Chavez called out a fake idol and is still here

Lewis Kelly handed Sharonda Cox a ring, called it a blood idol and said it tied their two games together for two Tribal Councils. Cristian Chavez pointed out that Lewis had already described the same ring to the tribe as a traditional Irish wedding ring. The one castaway nobody drafted has now outlasted seven who were drafted, and spent this episode doing the fact-checking.

What is left out, and why

The revote at Dina has no number here. Two outlets print a second 4–4, which cannot happen: a revote excludes the two tied castaways, leaving four voters, and extra votes do not carry into it. The stand-off that followed has no number either — every account agrees Kilby held for Lewis while the other three came across, but that is not the whole-tribe agreement the stated rule calls for, so the mechanism is described and the count is not. Savu's fourth ballot is uncredited for the same reason: one source, no corroboration. Paramount+ has now printed a wrong count in three of five weeks.
