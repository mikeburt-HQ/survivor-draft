The self-vote

Rob Antonson has a hidden immunity idol, which means Rob Antonson does not need to do anything clever. Rob Antonson decided to do something clever anyway.

The plan was to give Eric Macksoud a second chance by writing his own name down at Tribal Council. In the voting booth, a producer interrupted to explain that voting for yourself is against the rules, which is not a sentence this show has often needed.

Both halves of that scheme belong to Cagney. Rob is Cagney's. Eric, the man being rescued, is also Cagney's. One roster generated an entire rule clarification, and still finished the week having lost nobody at all.

Will pays this week's bill

Ana Sani volunteered to act as a decoy — deliberately drawing attention so the tribe could blindside Eric. The tribe then voted out the decoy. Alexis Levine carried word of Ana's name-throwing to Sharonda Cox and Linnea Capobianco, the plan curdled, and Ana went home instead of the person the plan was about.

Ana was Will's last pick, taken 18th. Two episodes, two eliminations, and both of them have come off the two rosters that lost someone first: Daron's, then Will's. Cagney and Mike have yet to be touched.

Daron's week could not have gone more differently

Last week she had every pick on the losing tribe and lost one of them. This week Toka was safe, Lewis Kelly came back from Exile and rejoined them, and Angelica “Jelly” Loblack won reward and found a hidden immunity idol described as having no drawbacks and no risks — just an idol.

Jelly told Patt Cannaday, and the alliance that formed out of it is Jelly, Patt, Devin Way, Brady Booker and Mike Pinsky. Three of those five are Daron's. She went from the worst possible draw to holding the idol and most of the group built around it in seven days.

Brady Booker is having a time

Week one: injured an achilles going too hard at the immunity challenge. Week two: severed roughly a quarter of a thumb while chopping wood. Medical cleared him to keep playing both times. Two episodes, two injuries, same person, and he is still in the game.

And Cristian Chavez, the head of HR nobody drafted, sat through a Tribal Council on Savu and came out the other side. He has now outlasted two people who were actually selected.
