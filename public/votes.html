Three things in the numbers

Danny Kilby has survived more votes than anyone. Five of the eleven votes cast in episode 3 carried Kilby's name, one short of the six that sent Patt Cannaday home. Kilby is Will's, sitting on Dina, and Dina keeps winning.

Jenna Doore is the only castaway written down at two different Tribals. Two votes in the premiere, four in episode 4, and that total of six ties Jenna with three castaways who were all voted out on the night they got theirs.

Cagney's five votes are spread across two castaways who both survived them. Eric Macksoud took four of ten in episode 2 and is still here; Alexis Levine took one.

Where these came from

Tallies are taken where at least two independent sources agree, then checked against the number of people who could actually vote that night. All four reconcile exactly.

Paramount+ has printed the wrong count twice

Weeks one and four both went out without a tally because the official recap disagreed with everyone else. It was the one that was wrong both times — 5–3 in the premiere against a 6–2 consensus, and a split totalling four votes on a five‑person tribe in episode 4. One other outlet printed episode 3 as 7–5, which cannot happen on a tribe of eleven. Episode 1 is the one number here resting on agreement between sources rather than on arithmetic, because 5–3 and 6–2 both add up to the eight votes available.
