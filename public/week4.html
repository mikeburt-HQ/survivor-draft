Mike now owns four sixths of a tribe

The shuffle produced a new Toka of six, and four of them are Mike's: Carter Krull, Mike Pinsky, Maggie Nestor and Thien An Nguyen. Brady Booker and Kristin Flickinger are the other two, which means a tribe of six contains a bloc of four people who all answer to the same drafter and have absolutely no idea.

It is the single luckiest thing that has happened to anyone in this draft. Mike did not arrange it, could not have arranged it, and has nothing to do with it. Toka also came second in the challenge, so nobody there had to vote.

Daron drew one of each

Daron has three castaways left and the swap put each of them on a different tribe. Jelly Loblack on Savu, Brady Booker on Toka, Lewis Kelly on Dina. One per buff, perfectly distributed, like a sampler plate.

The upside is that she cannot lose two in one Tribal Council. The downside is that she is now guaranteed to have somebody at every Tribal Council there is.

Will lost a third, and it was the one with the information

Savu came last, went to Tribal, and Jenna Doore went home. Devin Way and Jelly Loblack sided with Ori Jean-Charles and Alexis Levine instead — Jelly summed the choice up as the devil you know against the devil you don't.

Jenna is Will's third loss in four episodes, which takes him level with Daron on three. She was also the castaway who got tipped off in week one and swung that whole vote, so the person who redirected episode one and the person who did the tipping have now both gone, in consecutive episodes, from the same two rosters.

Jelly went into that Tribal holding an immunity idol and did not need to use it. She still has it.

Cristian Chavez is still here

The one castaway nobody drafted landed on Dina, won immunity with them, and has now outlasted five people who were actually picked. He is beating two of the four drafters on his own.
