Fire or water

The Million Dollar Coin Flip, as it played out

  1. At Tribal Council, one castaway could volunteer to flip a coin with fire on one side and water on the other, and call it.
  2. Call it right, and the season's prize goes from one million dollars to two.
  3. Call it wrong, and the flipper is out of the game on the spot — no vote.
  4. Devin Way and Lewis Kelly were the last two weighing it up. Lewis took the coin, called fire, and it came down water.

So the prize stays at one million, and the other million rolls over to next season. Nobody wrote anybody's name down. Lewis Kelly is the twelfth castaway to leave the game and the third member of the jury, but not the eleventh voted out, because there was no vote.

A rough night for pick number one

Lewis started the season alone on Exile and finished it holding a coin. In between, this week alone: won the reward challenge for the purple team on the slingshot, lasted past the half-hour mark in the immunity hang before dropping, and lost the necklace to Carter Krull. Then the flip.

Of the four first-round picks — Daron's Lewis Kelly, Will's Danny Kilby, Cagney's Alexis Levine and Mike's Carter Krull — Carter is now the only one still in the game. Carter is also the one who beat Lewis for immunity.

Daron and Will are down to one each

Daron's last castaway is Angelica “Jelly” Loblack. Will's is Sharonda Cox. Mike still has four of the nine and has not lost anyone since episode 5; Cagney has two. The jury now holds one castaway each from Will, Cagney and Daron, and none of Mike's.

The helicopter took both of Cagney's

The reward was a helicopter ride over Fiji and a fried-chicken meal, and it went to Mike Pinsky, Devin Way, Eric Macksoud, Lewis Kelly and Thien An Nguyen. Both of Cagney's castaways were on the winning side. Mike's four split two and two again, Daron's two were split, and Will's Sharonda Cox was on the losing side with the undrafted Cristian Chavez.

A fake idol, and a real one that is less secret

With Jelly's idol played last week, Devin Way used its old parchment and wrapping to build a fake idol and planted it for someone else to find. Separately, Cristian Chavez told Sharonda Cox about Carter Krull's idol — the real one, Rob's, still unplayed.

What is left out, and why

How Devin's attempt to steer Maggie Nestor to the fake idol ended is told four different ways — Maggie missed it, refused it, kept it after mistaking it for a leaf, or never got it — so the page does not say who has it. What a correct call would have won the flipper besides the extra million is described one way by three outlets and another by a fourth, so only the prize money is stated. One outlet calls Lewis the eleventh castaway voted off; no ballot was cast, and the page counts Lewis as eliminated, not voted out. The order everyone else dropped out of the immunity challenge is left out too. And the Tethered Idol has still not been mentioned since Kristin handed it to Sharonda, so its status is not stated.

Carried over from earlier weeks: the revote at Dina in episode 5 and the stand-off that settled it both still have no number, and Savu's fourth ballot that week is still uncredited.
