The idol is tethered to the wrong roster

Three castaways went to the summit, one from each tribe: Kristin Flickinger from Toka, Sharonda Cox from Dina, Ori Jean-Charles from Savu. Kristin volunteered to stay behind on Exile, which sent Sharonda and Ori back to their camps with a plate of ribs and corn and handed Kristin the Tethered Idol.

What the Tethered Idol does

  1. Only Kristin Flickinger may play it.
  2. It may be played only on Ori Jean-Charles or Sharonda Cox.
  3. It may never be played on Kristin.
  4. If both of those two are voted out while it is still in play, it becomes an ordinary hidden immunity idol, playable on anybody — Kristin included.

Kristin is Cagney's fourteenth pick. Ori was Will's tenth and Sharonda was Will's fifteenth, so every castaway the idol is permitted to save belongs to one rival — and to the drafter sitting last. Ori was voted out a few hours later, so the list is down to Sharonda alone. Cagney now holds an immunity idol whose only legal use is keeping Will in the game, and it only becomes freely usable once Will has nobody left for it to protect.

Savu is two people from two different rosters

Savu has now lost three immunity challenges in a row and been to three Tribal Councils in three episodes. It is down to Jelly Loblack for Daron and Devin Way for Cagney — one each, and nobody from Mike or Will. Jelly revealed the idol that has been in hand since episode 2 and told Devin the vote would not be going their way. Devin said Ori's pitch had flipped their brain, then wrote Ori's name down anyway.

A two-person tribe cannot really hold a vote: with one ballot each there is no majority to be had. So whatever happens to Savu next, it will not be decided the way the last three weeks were.

Two ballots from three people

Ori played a Shot in the Dark, drew Not Safe, and forfeited that night's vote. That is why three people at Tribal produced only two parchments, both of them reading Ori. It also settles an apparent disagreement in the sources: the episode table records 2–0 while the written summary says the vote was unanimous, and those are the same result counted two different ways.

The same rule produced this season's other odd tally. Episode 1 was eight ballots from ten people, because Aaliyah Puglia and Jenna Doore both played a Shot in the Dark and both drew Not Safe. Three Shots have now been played this season and all three came back Not Safe.

Will is down to two, and carrying 19 of the 48 ballots

Four of Will's five castaways have had a vote cast against them: Ana Sani, Jenna Doore, Danny Kilby and now Ori Jean-Charles. Only Sharonda Cox has never been written down. That roster has taken 19 of the 48 ballots cast this season — more than Cagney and Mike have taken put together, and nearly five times Mike's four.

Danny Kilby is still the castaway who has survived the most votes — five in episode 3, one short of the six that sent Patt Cannaday home. Kilby and Sharonda are what is left.

Toka has not been to Tribal Council since episode 3

Mike leads on four and holds four of Toka's six, and Toka has not voted anybody out in three episodes. Mike's only loss all season came from the one tribe Mike has nobody on: Linnea Capobianco went out at Dina in episode 5, armed by extra votes that Mike's own Maggie Nestor had handed over.

Cristian Chavez has now outlasted eight drafted castaways

The one castaway nobody picked on draft night is still on Dina, which has now won immunity two weeks running. Eight of the twenty who were drafted are out. Cristian is not.

What is left out, and why

Whether Toka or Dina finished first is not reported here. Three readings exist across the sources — Dina first, Toka first, and the two of them inside a few seconds of each other — so the page says only that both were safe. There is no reward on this page either: one source prints the reward as none while another describes a standing coffee kit for the top two finishers, and that is unresolved for this episode. And the Tethered Idol has no deadline here: a single outlet adds that Kristin must use it by the final five, and nothing corroborates that, so the four rules above are the ones every source agrees on.

Carried over from earlier weeks: the revote at Dina in episode 5 and the stand-off that settled it both still have no number, and Savu's fourth ballot that week is still uncredited.
