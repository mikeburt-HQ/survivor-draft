One necklace, and the right Mike is wearing it

The first individual immunity challenge of the season was a balance: stand on a narrowing beam and hold a whale statue up on a pole while the stages get longer. The last three were Mike Pinsky, Kristin Flickinger and Lewis Kelly. Kristin stepped off, then Lewis dropped, and Mike Pinsky took the necklace. The final three came from three different drafters, and the one who shares a name with a drafter won it.

Mike has four of the twelve still in the game, a third of the merged tribe, and has not lost anybody since episode 5.

Nine for Brady

The vote went 9–4. Brady Booker took nine, and Eric Macksoud took four and stayed. Nine is the most votes one castaway has taken at a single Tribal Council this season; the previous high was six. Brady was Daron's sixteenth pick, the eighth castaway voted out and the ninth to leave, and as of this episode the first member of the jury.

The rosters did not vote as blocks. All three of Cagney's castaways wrote Brady — Eric Macksoud included, the only other name on the night. Mike's four split down the middle: Mike Pinsky and Maggie Nestor wrote Brady, Carter Krull and Thien An Nguyen wrote Eric. Daron's three went two ways as well — Brady and Lewis Kelly wrote Eric, Jelly Loblack wrote Brady. Will's Danny Kilby and Sharonda Cox both wrote Brady, and so did the undrafted Cristian Chavez.

Ten at the feast, three back at camp

The merge feast went to the first ten to eat two grubs each. The three who missed it were Kristin Flickinger, Sharonda Cox and Cristian Chavez — one of Cagney's, one of Will's and the one nobody drafted. Every one of Mike's and Daron's castaways at the merge ate.

The Tethered Idol changed hands

Kristin Flickinger, who won the Tethered Idol on Exile last week, handed it to Sharonda Cox. So an idol that came off Cagney's roster now sits with one of Will's two remaining castaways — which, given that Sharonda was always the only person still alive it could be played on, at least saves everybody a walk.

Daron now carries the most votes

Brady's nine take Daron's roster to 25 of the season's 61 ballots, past Will's 19. Daron is down to two castaways, Lewis Kelly and Jelly Loblack. Four of Daron's five have now been written down; Jelly is the only one who never has.

Cristian Chavez has now outlasted nine drafted castaways

The one castaway nobody picked on draft night made the merge, missed the feast, voted with the majority, and is still here. Nine of the twenty who were drafted are not.

What is left out, and why

How each of the three missed the merge feast is told differently by different outlets, so the page says only that they missed it. One recap describes the reading of the votes as level at four each after eight were read; that is the order the parchments came out of the urn, not a tally, and the final count was nine to four. And the handover of the Tethered Idol is reported without its new terms: every outlet agrees Kristin gave it to Sharonda, but no two describe the same way who may now play it, or when.

Carried over from earlier weeks: the revote at Dina in episode 5 and the stand-off that settled it both still have no number, and Savu's fourth ballot that week is still uncredited.

Twelve left, one tribe, four known advantages. Mike's four are the biggest share of the merge, Daron's roster is carrying the most votes, and the one castaway nobody picked is still outlasting the ones everybody did.
