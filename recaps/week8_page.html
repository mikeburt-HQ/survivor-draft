Maggie and Mike Pinsky were the only pair drafted by one person — Mike, naturally. Mike has a castaway in three of the six pairs.

Kilby & Kristin and Maggie & Mike were the first two pairs out of the challenge. Carter & Cristian won the first stage, and Lewis & Sharonda won the whole thing, immunity and reward, and took Jelly and Kristin along to eat.

How ten ballots became one

Twelve people went to Tribal Council and ten ballots were cast, because Kristin Flickinger and Cristian Chavez each played a Shot in the Dark and gave up that night's vote. Kristin's came back Not Safe. Cristian's came back Safe. Five Shots in the Dark have now been played this season, and Cristian's is the only one that has ever come back Safe.

Eight ballots went against Devin Way & Jelly Loblack, and Jelly played an idol that cancelled all eight. Jelly's own ballot went against Carter Krull & Cristian Chavez, and Cristian's Safe Shot cancelled that one. That left a single ballot that counted: Devin's, for Danny Kilby & Kristin Flickinger. The final count was one to nothing to nothing.

Cagney voted out Cagney

Devin is Cagney's nineteenth pick. Kristin was Cagney's fourteenth. So the one vote that counted all week was cast by Cagney's castaway against Cagney's castaway, and it took Will's Danny Kilby — Will's very first pick — out with it.

Devin was not the only one writing down a teammate. Eric Macksoud, also Cagney's, voted against Devin; Lewis Kelly, Daron's, voted against Daron's own Jelly. Neither of those counted. All four of Mike's castaways voted against Devin & Jelly, so half of the eight ballots that idol cancelled came off Mike's roster.

Will is down to Sharonda

Four of Will's five are gone. Sharonda Cox, the last, has never had a vote cast against Sharonda's name, held immunity this week with Lewis, and is still carrying the Tethered Idol that Kristin handed over last week. Kilby goes out having survived five votes in episode 3 and then left on one.

The jury lost a member before it met

Brady Booker, voted out last week, asked to leave the game to look after an injured thumb and gave up the jury seat. So the jury is Kilby and Kristin — one of Will's and one of Cagney's.

Cristian Chavez had the busiest week of anyone

The undrafted castaway won the first stage with Carter, was sent on the journey, arrived without a vote, won it back and an extra vote on top, saw that extra vote end up with Eric Macksoud (who did not use it), played the season's first Safe Shot in the Dark, and took a first-ever vote that did not count. Cristian has now outlasted eleven drafted castaways.
