Things That Happened

Pattern-spotting only — no takes on who drafted well, and nothing about how any of this plays out.

Will quietly took all of Canada

The cast has exactly two Canadians, Danny Kilby and Ana Sani, both out of Ontario. Will got both — one with his first pick, one with his last.

Prosecution and defense went separate ways

Cagney took criminal defense attorney Alexis Levine at #3. Daron took federal prosecutor Patt Cannaday at #9. They will be on opposite rosters all season.

Mike cornered the farm vote

Three people in this cast farm for a living. Daron took the Irish one at #1, and Mike took the other two — livestock farmer Carter Krull and Maggie Nestor.

A full decade separates the rosters

Cagney's five average 38.0, Daron's 28.2. Cagney also holds the oldest castaway in the cast, 49-year-old Kristin Flickinger.

Mike took three of the four youngest

Carter Krull and Thien An Nguyen are both 24, Linnea Capobianco is 25. The only other castaway under 26 is Aaliyah Puglia, who went to Daron.

Rhode Island reports to Cagney

Both castaways who grew up in Rhode Island — Eric Macksoud and Rob Antonson — landed on the same roster. Mike also drafted a Mike at #5.
